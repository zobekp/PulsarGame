DPI Lab

Find the mouse DPI you actually aim best at. You play the same drill at several simulated DPIs (in shuffled order) and the app scores each one.

The browser can't change your mouse's hardware DPI, so DPI Lab scales raw mouse deltas instead: at your in-game sensitivity, a simulated 1600 DPI moves the crosshair exactly twice as far per inch as 800. Results are also reported as eDPI and cm/360, so you can change either DPI or in-game sens afterwards.
